🛰️ Mission Brief
> *Bridging engineering rigor with real-world impact — from concept to orbit.*
I am Sadiq Ali Mir, an Aerospace Engineer specialising in systems engineering, driven by the challenge of building complex, high-impact aerospace systems from concept to realisation.
As Founder & CEO of Zephyra Dynamics, I am working to shape urban air mobility in India through autonomous eVTOL aircraft and intelligent airspace management. As an Astronaut Candidate with Titans Space Industries, I am equally committed to advancing human spaceflight and reusable access to space.
